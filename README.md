Decoupled Dual-Lens and Deep Vacuum Architecture

To eliminate the massive structural force of atmospheric pressure (~700 kg on a 300mm surface), the system splits the optical path into two distinct blocks, ensuring high internal heat trapping without structural collapse.

Flow: [Solar Rays] -> [Primary PMMA Lens] -> (Air Cone) -> [Quartz Vacuum Lens] -> (Vacuum Core) -> Target Plate

Stage 1 (Primary Atmospheric Collector): An external PMMA Fresnel Lens (250mm-350mm per module) that intercepts raw sunlight. It operates at frozen ambient outdoor temperatures under zero mechanical or thermal strain.

Stage 2 (Vacuum Pressure Boundary): A compact, high-density Fused Quartz (SiO2) or Borosilicate lens (40mm-50mm, thickness >= 6mm). Shrunk to a minute aperture, the atmospheric crushing force drops to a manageable ~16 kg, which is safely handled by standard 2-inch heavy-wall carbon steel or brass pipe housings.

The Core Vacuum Isolation: The internal thermal chamber behind the quartz window is drawn down to a deep structural vacuum (<= 10^-3 Pa). By removing air gas molecules, convective and conductive heat leaks to the external winter atmosphere are driven to absolute zero.

Non-Flammable Fluid Dynamics and Edge-AI Flow Control

The system completely rejects hazardous, high-vapor-pressure volatile hydrocarbons (such as kerosene) due to catastrophic flash-fire and explosion risks at high temperatures.

Dielectric Heat Transfer Medium: The system utilizes High-Temperature Polymethylsiloxane (Silicone Fluid / PMX-200 series) or Perfluoropolyether (PFPE Galden matrix). These fluids are structurally non-flammable, exhibit zero vapor pressure expansion at +300C, remain highly fluid at -40C, and are entirely non-corrosive to copper lines.

The Absorption Layer and Copper Heat-Sink: The solar ray bundle hits a 15mm thick Oxygen-Free High-Conductivity (OFHC) Copper disk treated with a Magnetron-Sputtered TiNOx Multi-Layer Coating (absorption >= 0.95, emission <= 0.04). This acts as a radiative thermal mirror. A continuous CNC-bent 10mm seamless copper line is vacuum-brazed to the rear face, sweeping the +300C fluid into the domestic space-heating layout.

Edge-AI Predictive Loop: A microcontroller computing layer (Embedded PID + Extended Kalman Filter) monitors the thermal sensors at 50 Hz. It modulates the brushless fluid pump speed via a variable PWM rail, predicting cloud occlusion and dynamically locking the core heat transfer matrix at exactly +300C.

Passive Anti-Thermosiphon and Autonomous Drainback Protocol

Flow: [Gross Solar Input] -> [Edge-AI Sensor Check] -> [Sunlight Active Mode] -> Pump ON -> Circulation
Or: [Cloud / Night Interclusion] -> Pump OFF -> Fluid Gravity-Drain -> To House

To prevent the outdoor collector from reversing its function and dumping the house's stored heat back into the cold sky during nighttime or cloudy phases, the system runs an automated safety routine:

The Anti-Thermosiphon Lock: A spring-loaded stainless steel non-return check valve paired with a normally-closed (NC) 12V solenoid valve dead-locks the line the moment the AI registers a core temperature drop.

The Drainback Routine: All external copper runs maintain a downward slope of >= 2 degrees. Upon pump de-energization, the silicone thermal fluid completely evacuates the outdoor chassis, dropping under earth gravity into a protected reservoir tank inside the warm habitat. The outdoor array stays dry and safe from freezing or boiling away.
